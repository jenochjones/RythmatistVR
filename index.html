Tutorial

How to play Rithmatist VR.

Controls

Hold a triggeryour controller becomes a piece of chalk, held like a pencil in your hand: the controller stays visible, and a longer pencil sits in it, blunt end at the controller and tip forward, left, and below it, angled 45° down and 20° to the left. It only draws when the tip is within 6 inches of the ground, and the lines are thin (about 2 cm wide).
Wait 1 second to activatedrawings stay plain white chalk for 1 second after you finish drawing them — then their lines glow gold and they come alive, even if you start drawing something else right away. Completed drawings are not filled in: just the lines, glowing gold.
Cloth in the other handhold chalk in one hand (trigger held), then press the trigger on the other hand — that hand gets a light-blue cloth, not a second piece of chalk. Rub the cloth on the ground over one of your own drawings to erase it: pending white chalk, Chalklings, defenses, defenders, tethers, and walls you drew. It never erases your foe's drawings, and it only erases when the cloth is near the ground. On desktop, Shift + drag erases instead of drawing.
MoveVR thumbstick, or WASD on desktop. Q / E turn, right-drag looks around on desktop. On desktop, drag with the mouse to draw.
Winbest of 3 rounds. Win a round by dropping your foe to 0 health. First to 2 rounds wins the match.

Defenses

First: draw your defense all the way around yourselfa closed shape that encloses you becomes your initial defense. A circle is strongest — the closer to a perfect circle, the more health it has (a perfect circle has 1000).
Draw inside your perimeteryou cannot leave your first perimeter, so draw everything inside it. Any closed shape drawn inside it that does not connect to a defense line becomes a Chalkling, and on activation it wiggles out through your own perimeter to attack your foe — your own defense never blocks your own Chalklings.
You cannot leave your first perimeteronce your initial defense exists, you can move only inside it, sliding along its inside edge. If it breaks, you can move freely again until you draw a new one.
Later defenses must physically connect to a defense linea closed shape becomes part of your defense only if its drawn line actually touches (within about 15 cm of) a perimeter defense line. Nearby is not enough — if it does not connect, it is a Chalkling, not a defense.
Tethered defendersdraw a line out from your defense (a string/chain), then a closed shape near its far end. That defender hunts enemy Chalklings, but cannot go further than its tether allows.
Long straight line (not at your foe)becomes a Line of Forbiddance — a wall Chalklings must break through.

Chalklings

Anything closed and not connected to a defense is a Chalklingit wiggles off to attack your foe's defenses, then your foe.
Strength comes from the drawinglong and thin is fast but weak — a straight dart at your foe travels at 3.4 m/s, does 10 damage a hit, and erodes after 100 damage, so 100 hits break a perfect circle. A perfect circle is very slow but does 80 damage/s and lasts 800 damage. More complex shapes sit in between.
Centre line = pathdraw a line down through a Chalkling and it will follow that line, scaled up to fit between you and your foe. With no centre line, it meanders generally toward your foe.
Attacking a defensea Chalkling meanders along the defense, slowly erasing it, and does extra damage at a corner or dip. A perfect circle has no corners, so it just takes slight damage all the way around. Chalklings erode as they deal damage and disappear when their budget is spent.
No picking up Chalklingsyou cannot pick up, carry, or throw a Chalkling — in VR, on desktop, or in a duel. Once it activates, it acts on its own. Squeezing a controller does nothing. You affect Chalklings only indirectly, by drawing defenses, defenders, walls, or new Chalklings.
Chalklings have no eyesa Chalkling is just its gold glowing lines and a thin owner-coloured line — no face, no fill.
Chalk is a resourcedrawing costs chalk, and it recharges over time. You can have at most 5 Chalklings alive at once.

Testing Mode

Testing Modeyour opponent stands still and does not draw — no defenses, no Chalklings, no attacks. Use it to try out drawings, test how a shape moves and hits, and see how much damage it does, without anything coming back at you.

Scenery and VR

Scenery rotates each matcheach time you start a match, the set changes: The Stage, then The Park, then The Roman Coliseum, then back to The Stage. In a duel, the host's set is used so both players see the same scenery.
On Questopen this page in the Meta Quest Browser, then tap Enter VR. After a game starts, the Enter VR button stays in the bottom-right corner, so you can switch from 2D to VR at any time, and Exit VR brings you back to 2D.
On desktopdrag with the mouse to draw, WASD to move, Q / E to turn, right-drag to look around.
