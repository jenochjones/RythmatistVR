Rithmatist VR

Duel of Chalklings — a tournament duel, drawn in chalk.

You and your opponent start 100 feet apart in a chalk arena. Hold a trigger and your controller becomes a piece of chalk. Draw on the ground, and your drawings come alive.

Closed shapebecomes a Chalkling — it wiggles toward your foe and attacks their defences.
Big circle around youbecomes a Line of Warding — your defensive ring.
Long straight linebecomes a Line of Forbiddance — a wall Chalklings must break through.
Grip / Squeezegrab a nearby Chalkling, then throw it toward your foe.
Thumbstick / WASDmove around your half of the arena. Win a round by dropping your foe to 0 health. First to 2 rounds wins the match.

Share this code with your opponent. They open this same page, choose Join a Duel, and enter it.

Desktop test mode: drag with the mouse to draw, WASD to move, Q / E to turn. On Quest, open this page in the Meta Quest Browser, then tap Enter VR. After a game starts, the Enter VR button stays in the bottom-right corner, so you can switch from 2D to VR at any time.
