Rithmatist VR

Duel of Chalklings — a tournament duel, drawn in chalk.

You and your opponent start 100 feet apart in a chalk arena. Hold a trigger and your controller becomes a piece of chalk. Draw on the ground, and your drawings come alive.

First: draw your defense all the way around yourselfa closed shape that encloses you becomes your initial defense. A circle is strongest — the closer to a perfect circle, the more health it has (a perfect circle has 1000). You cannot leave this first perimeter: once it exists, you can move only inside it, sliding along its inside edge. If it breaks, you can move freely again until you draw a new one.
Scenery rotates each matcheach time you start a match, the set changes: The Stage, then The Park, then The Roman Coliseum, then back to The Stage. In a duel, the host's set is used so both players see the same scenery.
Chalklings have no eyesa Chalkling is just its gold shape and owner-coloured outline — no face.
Later defenses must touch that first onea closed shape attached to your initial defense extends it. Anything closed and not attached is a Chalkling, and wiggles off to attack.
Tethered defendersdraw a line out from your defense (a string/chain), then a closed shape near its far end. That defender hunts enemy Chalklings, but cannot go further than its tether allows.
Chalkling strength comes from the drawinglong and thin is fast but weak — a straight dart at your foe travels at 3.4 m/s, does 10 damage a hit, and erodes after 100 damage, so 100 hits break a perfect circle. A perfect circle is very slow but does 80 damage/s and lasts 800 damage. More complex shapes sit in between.
Centre line = pathdraw a line down through a Chalkling and it will follow that line, scaled up to fit between you and your foe. With no centre line, it meanders generally toward your foe.
Attacking a defensea Chalkling meanders along the defense, slowly erasing it, and does extra damage at a corner or dip. A perfect circle has no corners, so it just takes slight damage all the way around. Chalklings erode as they deal damage and disappear when their budget is spent.
Long straight line (not at your foe)becomes a Line of Forbiddance — a wall Chalklings must break through.
No picking up Chalklingsyou cannot pick up, carry, or throw a Chalkling — in VR, on desktop, or in a duel. Once it activates, it acts on its own. Squeezing a controller does nothing. You affect Chalklings only indirectly, by drawing defenses, defenders, walls, or new Chalklings.
Chalk points down, tip near the groundthe chalk sticks out below the controller. It only draws when the tip is within 6 inches of the ground, and the lines are thin.
Lift for 3 seconds to activatedrawings stay plain white chalk until you have lifted the chalk for 3 seconds straight — then they glow gold and come alive.
Thumbstick / WASDmove around your half of the arena. Win a round by dropping your foe to 0 health. First to 2 rounds wins the match.
